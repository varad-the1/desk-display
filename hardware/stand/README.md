# Tablet stand (optional)

A one-piece, open-frame stand that holds a 10.1" tablet in landscape at 22 degrees back from vertical,
with a small platform behind it for a speaker and snap-in clips for the cables. It is designed to use as little filament as possible.

* `stand.scad` is the editable OpenSCAD source (tablet size, lean angle, platform depth and clip sizes are parameters at the top).
  Defaults are for a 242 x 168 x 8.1 mm tablet; add the thickness of a case to `case`.
* `stand.stl` is ready to print: about 197 x 138 x 107 mm, no supports, flat on the bed.
* Rough use of material: about 33 cm3 solid, roughly 25-30 g with 3 walls and 8-10% infill. PETG is better than PLA for the cable clips.
* Print one clip on its own first to check that your cables snap in.

`preview_side.png` and `preview_iso.png` show the stand with a tablet and an example speaker block.
