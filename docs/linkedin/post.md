I turned an old Android tablet into a smart desk display. It's open source.

No cloud account, no framework: one HTML page inside a small Android kiosk app that boots straight into it.

What it does:
- Clock, alarms with a volume ramp, timers, stopwatch, world clocks, Pomodoro
- Weather, a calendar (tap any date for its events), daily routine
- Shows whatever the tablet is playing, with a live visualiser and synced lyrics
- 16 famous paintings as the idle screen, with a bit of trivia on each
- Auto night mode, a phone remote protected by a PIN, and updates over Wi-Fi

The lyrics screen comes up after 10 seconds of music, the paintings after 2 minutes. Spotify is optional: everything works with any music app, and Premium only unlocks alarm songs.

Built without Gradle, using a plain PowerShell build script, and designed around the limits of a very old WebView.
